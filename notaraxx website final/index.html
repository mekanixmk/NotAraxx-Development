The founder of NotAraxx Development is only fifteen years old, and leads the group from India with one belief: age is not a limit, effort is. As a leader, he builds teams, takes responsibility, and never stops learning. A music enthusiast at heart, he started NotAraxx Music, where sound and creativity come together. He is also a passionate gamer who values the strategy and teamwork that gaming teaches. Through NotAraxx Technologies, he builds digital products and teaches financial literacy, because the goal is not to work for money, it is to make money work for you. And with NotAraxx AI, the AI the group develops, he is building smart tools that help people learn and create. Welcome to NotAraxx Development.
